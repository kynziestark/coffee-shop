Your neighborhood coffee break starts here.

Welcome to Willow & Bean Coffee Co., a fictional locally owned coffee shop imagined for the heart of Iowa City. We believe a great cup of coffee can make an ordinary morning feel special.

Whether you are meeting a friend, grabbing a latte before class, or settling in with a good book, there is a place for you here.

Explore Our Coffee

A little coffee, a lot of comfort

Founded in our fictional story by friends Maya Bennett and Lucas Reed, Willow & Bean was designed around a simple idea: coffee shops should feel welcoming to everyone. Our imagined menu pairs carefully prepared espresso drinks with fresh pastries and seasonal favorites.

Enjoy a smooth drip coffee, a rich espresso, or a creamy latte made to fit your morning routine.
