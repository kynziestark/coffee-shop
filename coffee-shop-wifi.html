A Workspace That Feels Like a Coffee Shop

Wi-Fi for Getting Things Done

Our fictional café concept includes customer Wi-Fi for browsing course materials, checking email, and working on everyday online tasks.

Comfortable Laptop Seating

Picture individual tables, cushioned chairs, and a few shared work surfaces for students and remote workers who need room to spread out their materials.

Convenient Power Outlets

Great for Students and Remote Workers

A change of scenery can make a long work session feel more manageable. Willow & Bean is imagined as a welcoming option for University of Iowa students, freelancers, and professionals who prefer working outside their homes.

What to Order for Your Study Session

Our imagined menu includes drinks and snacks that fit different study routines. Choose a classic drip coffee for a long reading session, an iced latte for an afternoon pick-me-up, or a pastry when you need a break.

  • Drip coffee: a simple everyday favorite.
